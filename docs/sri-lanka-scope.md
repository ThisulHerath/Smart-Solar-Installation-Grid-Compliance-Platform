# Sri Lankan scope and assumptions

This university product supports Sri Lankan rooftop-solar planning: LKR equipment estimates, local property details, single/three-phase measurements, staff review, and equipment procurement preparation.

PUCSL describes Net Metering, Net Accounting, Net Plus and Net Plus Plus on https://www.pucsl.gov.lk/rooftop-solar-pv-connection-schemes/. Utility agreements and tariffs depend on the applicable scheme and agreement; this app does not calculate feed-in revenue, submit a utility application, certify anti-islanding or issue connection approval. Guidance is at https://www.pucsl.gov.lk/electricity/consumer/domestic-solar-power/.

The following are transparent project assumptions, not statutory limits: sizing monthly kWh / 120; preliminary 400 W panel estimate; 230/400 V nominal with +/-6% screening; 49.5-50.5 Hz screening; and additional review above 10 kW. The final equipment catalog policy uses 500 W panels, so final panel count differs from preliminary sizing. Engineering drawings, shading, structure, cable/protection design, export capacity and scheme choice require professional verification.

Prices in demo records are synthetic catalog examples. Live exchange conversion is real, but supplier prices must be entered and maintained by the inventory officer. Equipment estimates exclude installation, taxes, freight, labour, financing and tariff income.
